How to read it

The shape is the scheduler.

Steep curve

A large sigma interval is crossed in one step. The scheduler is spending less of its budget there.

Flat curve

Many evaluations cover a small sigma range. That part of the trajectory gets more of the step budget.

Δsigma

Turns the same schedule into per-step movement. Large peaks show where the biggest jumps occur.

Log + 0

Expands the low-noise tail while keeping the real terminal zero visible with a short linear zone below 1e-3.

Method

Pinned data, browser-rendered charts.

The Python generator reimplements the small scheduler formulas and verifies them against a pinned ComfyUI checkout. It produces the complete dataset for steps 2–50.

The live charts are rendered at device resolution in the browser with HTML Canvas. They are not screenshots or pre-rendered SVGs, so resizing, hovering, filtering, and changing steps all operate on the underlying data.
